WhatsApp Business Integration Guide

Connect your Meta WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) to Yield.ai in 5 simple steps.

1

Meta Business Manager Verification

Ensure you have an active Facebook/Meta Business Suite account with administrator access. Meta requires business verification to send unlimited WhatsApp messages to new leads.

  • Business Tax ID or Registration Document
  • Official Business Website or Domain Email
2

Meta Embedded Signup Flow

Navigate to the Connect WhatsApp page and click "Log in with Facebook". This launches Meta's official Embedded Signup modal.

Permissions Requested by Yield.ai:

• whatsapp_business_management — Configure webhooks and business profile

• whatsapp_business_messaging — Receive incoming buyer inquiries and send AI responses

3

Phone Number Registration

Select an existing WhatsApp Business Phone Number or register a dedicated new number. You will receive an SMS or Voice OTP code from Meta to verify ownership.

Note: If the phone number is currently active on standard WhatsApp Mobile App, you must delete the account from the phone before linking to the Business Cloud API.
4

Customize AI Broker Identity & System Prompt

In your Client Dashboard under AI Settings, set your AI persona details (Agent Name, Brokerage Brand, Tone of Voice, Qualification Guidelines, and Viewing Scheduler Rules).

5

Live Test & Outbound Simulation

Once connected, visit the Bayut Outbound Tool to simulate incoming leads and verify instant WhatsApp auto-responses and CRM lead tracking.
